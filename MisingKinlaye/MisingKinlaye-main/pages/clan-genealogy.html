Genealogy of Mising Clans

The genealogy of the Mising people traces its ancestral origin to Abotani ————— "The First Human." In Mising tradition, "Abo" means Father and "Tani" means Human, representing the ancestral figure from whom the lineage of humanity is believed to have begun.

This section is dedicated to documenting and preserving the genealogical histories of Mising clans. Many clan genealogies are still under research and documentation, and additional records will be added as more historical information becomes available.
