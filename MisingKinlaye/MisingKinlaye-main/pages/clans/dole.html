Dole: Opín

Dole: is commonly written as Doley among the Mising people.

According to traditional genealogical accounts and migration records, the Dole: clan is believed to have undergone a division in the northeastern region of present-day Arunachal Pradesh in the past.

Following this separation, one group is believed to have migrated towards the western region, where they are now associated with areas inhabited by the Galo people. Another group is believed to have lived alongside the Pe:gu clan in the region of Pegu Siyeng.

Over time, both branches of the Dole: clan are believed to have continued their migration southward and eventually settled in present-day Assam, where their descendants continue to preserve their ancestral identity and clan lineage.
