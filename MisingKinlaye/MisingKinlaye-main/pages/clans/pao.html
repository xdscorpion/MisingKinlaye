Pao Opín

Pao is one of the clans of the Mising tribe, with a distinct genealogical lineage that forms an important part of Mising ancestral history. According to the genealogical traditions and lineage records associated with the clan, the Pao clan is believed to have descended from the Minyong group.

Genealogy of Pao Opín

In the original document containing the Pao genealogy, the names of the ancestors preceding Do:lo had been forgotten or omitted. However, the owner of this website independently researched and connected the genealogical records of different clans to trace and identify the names of the ancestors who came before Do:lo.
