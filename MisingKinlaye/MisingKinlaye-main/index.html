What is MisingKinlaye?

Mising Kinla:yé literally means "Let's Learn Mising." As the name of this website, it represents an open invitation to learn about the Mising people— their history, culture, traditions, genealogy, customs and heritage—while preserving this knowledge for future generations.

An Initiative of

Takam Mising Sí:sang Kébang

This website is an initiative of the members of Takam Mising Sí:sang Kébang (TMSK), committed to promoting, documenting and preserving the history, identity and heritage of the Mising people.
