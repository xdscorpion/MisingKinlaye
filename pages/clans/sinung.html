Sinung Érang

According to the genealogical tradition, Sinung was the son of Abo Pu:si, who had three sons: Sidong, Sinung, and Sian.

The Pa:tír, Pa:me, Pa:nyang, Pa:dun, and Pasar clans are considered to belong to the lineage of Abo Sinung.

Before becoming part of the broader Mising fold, the Pa:tír and Pa:nyang clans were traditionally identified as belonging to the Panggi subgroup, while the Pa:me clan was associated with the Bori subgroup.

Note: The Bori subgroup referred to here is distinct from the Bori clan of the Mising people. The subgroup mentioned in this context continues to exist among the Adi people, where it is recognised as an Adi subgroup. It should therefore not be confused with the Bori clan found within the Mising community.

Over time, some clan names within this lineage have developed different spellings within the Mising community:

  • Pa:tír is commonly written as Patir among the Mising people.
  • Pa:me is commonly written as Pamegam,Pame or Pamey among the Mising people.
